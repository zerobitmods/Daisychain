# Hello, and welcome to Daisychain!

Let's get into some pipes! Daisychain covers all your logistical needs, from your very first pipe all the way to a mega-factory.
Items, fluids and energy will always find their place in any system you design.

We also built it to be kind to your server. Pipes sit quietly doing nothing until you ask one to pull, so feel free to go big. Your
TPS will be fine. Probably happier than you, honestly.

**Handy trick:** hold <KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" /> while hovering over any Daisychain item and this guide jumps straight to its
page.
