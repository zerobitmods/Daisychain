# The Bloom Wrench

Say hello to your new best friend! The <ItemLink id="daisychain:bloom_wrench" /> is how you tell each side of a pipe what to do. Point at a
side, sneak and right-click, and it changes its mode. A plain right-click opens the pipe's settings, just like an empty hand.

While you're sneaking with it, the side you're looking at shows a see-through ghost arm, so you always know which side you're
about to change. Light blue means it's open, and red means it's switched off. Hologram in the way of the part you want? Sneak
and right-click anything that isn't a pipe (a block or just the air) to turn the holograms off, and do it again to bring them back.
With them off, you change a side by clicking the pipe's own face on that side.

## What each sneak-click does

It depends on what the side is touching:

| Side touches | Each click goes |
| --- | --- |
| A block | Off → Pull → Insert → Pull → Off … |
| Air | Off ↔ Pull |
| Another pipe | Connected ↔ Off |

Next to a block, **Pull is always one click away**, whichever end you're starting from. From Insert, one click gives you Pull, and
the next takes you to Off. It bounces back and forth like that forever.

You can even make a pull side that faces nothing at all. It'll wait there patiently, and as soon as you place a <ItemLink id="minecraft:chest" /> in front of it, it gets to work.

Switching a pipe-to-pipe connection off splits the two pipes apart, which is a handy way to turn one long line into two separate
networks.

## Already got a favorite wrench?

Bring it along! Wrenches from most other mods work too, as long as the mod marks them as a wrench.
