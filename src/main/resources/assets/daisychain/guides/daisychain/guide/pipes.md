# The pipes

Meet the family! There are four kinds of pipe, and each one only connects to its own kind (energy pipes of every tier count as
one kind). That means you can run an item line right next
to a fluid line and they'll politely ignore each other.

| Pipe | Color | Carries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <ItemLink id="daisychain:item_pipe" /> | Gray | Items |
| <ItemLink id="daisychain:fluid_pipe" /> | Blue | Fluids |
| <ItemLink id="daisychain:energy_pipe" /> | Dark green, with a colored band | Energy (FE), in six tiers (see [Energy pipe tiers](energy-pipes.md)) |
| <ItemLink id="daisychain:universal_pipe" /> | Black | All three at once |

They all get along with blocks from other mods too, as long as the block has a normal inventory, tank or battery.

## The Universal Pipe

The overachiever of the family. One pipe carries items, fluids and energy at the same time, each at its own speed. Connect a single
line to a row of machines and it can feed them, fill them and power them all at once.

Each side can switch items, fluids or energy off by itself. Got a machine that should get power from the line but no items? Turn
items off on that side and it'll only get the power.

## Built to last

Pipes break in a blink with anything, even your bare hands, so rearranging is painless. They also shrug off water and lava, don't
budge for pistons, and are as blast resistant as stone. And the <ItemLink id="daisychain:energy_pipe" /> never hangs on to energy
itself: it goes straight from the source to the machine.
